🔬 Abstract  
Influenza is a highly contagious viral disease with diverse manifestations, particularly in pediatric populations. This study investigates the clinical presentation of influenza in children, focusing on the occurrence of viral exanthem and the effectiveness of various fever management strategies. A total of 250 pediatric patients were observed, with 137 showing clinical signs of influenza. The study identified five major syndromes: intoxication, hyperthermic, catarrhal, respiratory, and viral exanthem. The effectiveness of pharmacological treatments (paracetamol, ibuprofen) was compared with physical methods of temperature reduction, such as cooling compresses and increased fluid intake. The findings emphasize the importance of individualized treatment strategies and the role of viral exanthem as a key diagnostic clue, while optimizing fever management to reduce complications and unnecessary medical interventions.
